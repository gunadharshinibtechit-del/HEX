How HEX works

HEX stands for Human-in-the-Loop Explainability. The goal is to help people understand an AI suggestion instead of blindly trusting a black box.

✦
One important idea

AI can support a decision by showing evidence. A human should review the evidence and remain responsible for the final choice.

TECHNICAL NOTE

What is implemented in this prototype?

  • Python + Flask: routes and server-side logic for each page.
  • SQLite: saves applicants and review actions locally, even after restarting the app.
  • Preference-weighted demo score: combines three values using simple weights to demonstrate explainability.
  • Human-in-the-loop: records the reviewer's choice and optional reason.
!

Not implemented yet: a trained Deep Reinforcement Learning agent, real AI model, authentication, fairness validation or a real scholarship eligibility system. The score is for demonstrating the concept only.
